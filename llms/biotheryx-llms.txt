# BioTheryX

> BioTheryX is a private, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in San Diego, California, developing first-in-class targeted protein degraders — bifunctional degraders and molecular glues — for cancer and inflammatory disease, on its proprietary PRODEGY Cereblon-based platform.
